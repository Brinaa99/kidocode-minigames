HOW TO PLAY

1. READ THE MISSION

Check the mission banner: Shoot the Larger Angle, Smaller Angle, or Equal Angle.

2. COMPARE ANGLE ARCS

Observe the ray opening arcs and degree values displayed on each target stand to identify the correct angle.

3. AIM & SHOOT

Rotate your bow with A / D, Arrow Keys, or Mouse Drag. Follow the trajectory guide and press SPACE or tap SHOOT!

4. BUILD STREAKS

Hit targets consecutively to boost your score multiplier up to 10x Streak! Watch out for moving targets and wind in later rounds.
