✖️ 1. MULTIPLICATION TABLES & PRODUCTS
A product is the result of multiplying two factors together.
Example: In 7 × 8 = 56, 7 and 8 are factors, and 56 is the product.
🔢 2. MULTIPLES
Multiples are the numbers you get by skip-counting or multiplying by whole numbers.
Example: Multiples of 4 are 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24...
A common multiple is a number that appears in multiple sequences (e.g. 12 is a common multiple of 4 and 6).
🧩 3. FACTORS & FACTOR PAIRS
Factors are numbers that divide evenly into another number with no remainder.
Example: Factors of 24 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24.
Factor pairs for 24 are (1×24), (2×12), (3×8), and (4×6).
