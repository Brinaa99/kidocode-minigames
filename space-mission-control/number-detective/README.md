# THE NUMBER DETECTIVE 🕵️‍♂️🔍
**Building: Multiplication, Multiples and Factors // Room: 1 – Tables, Multiples and Factors**

An interactive 16-bit retro pixel-art detective mystery game designed for primary school students to master multiplication facts, multiples, and factors through immersive crime scene investigations.

---

## 🔍 Game Concept & Story

The city is puzzled by a series of mysterious numerical incidents: broken ledgers, secret radio frequencies, misplaced warehouse crates, and cipher-locked safes left by the elusive "Phantom Factor".

As a Junior Investigator at the **Central Number Detective Bureau**, the player investigates crime scenes, uses the detective magnifying glass, records clues in their detective notebook, analyzes mathematical evidence, and eliminates false suspects to crack all 10 cases!

---

## 📜 10 Detective Cases

1. **Case 1 – The Missing Number:** The Archivist's torn ledger (Multiplication facts: 7×8=56, 9×6=54, [?]×6=48).
2. **Case 2 – The Secret Sequence:** The subway platform multiples trail (Multiples of 4, 7, and 6).
3. **Case 3 – The Warehouse Mystery:** The harbor docks equal groupings (Factors of 24 and 30).
4. **Case 4 – The Suspicious Suspects:** Lineup elimination using multiplication facts (7×6=42, 8×9=72, 9×7=63).
5. **Case 5 – The Stolen Collection:** Museum heist common multiples (Common multiples of 3 and 5, and 4 and 6).
6. **Case 6 – The Locked Evidence Room:** Security vault combination locks using factor pairs (36 and 48).
7. **Case 7 – The Double Agent:** Intercepting covert clock tower transmissions (Lowest Common Multiples: LCM of 4 & 6, 3 & 5).
8. **Case 8 – The Broken Machine:** Professor Cipher's mechanical decryption cogs (Product-factor matching: 56, 72, 63).
9. **Case 9 – The Master Code:** Triple-layer cyber encryption (Multiplication, multiples, and factor counts).
10. **Case 10 – The Final Investigation:** Showdown with the Phantom Factor at City Hall (Full Room 1 Mastery).

---

## 🛠️ Detective Tools & Architecture

- **Magnifying Glass:** Inspect objects on canvas to uncover hidden mathematical clues.
- **Detective Notebook:** Automatically logs all verified mathematical evidence and rules.
- **Detective Badge & Ranks:** Junior Investigator ➔ Senior Sleuth ➔ Lead Inspector ➔ Master Number Detective 🌟.
- **Web Audio API:** Zero external audio dependencies. Generates authentic retro typewriter clicks, evidence chimes, thumbtack pops, safe tumbler clicks, and mystery victory fanfares.
- **State Persistence:** LocalStorage tracking of cases solved, stars, and total bureau score.
