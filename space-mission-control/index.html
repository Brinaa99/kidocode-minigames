SPACE COMMAND ACADEMY

Learn the 5 likelihood terms used by Space Mission Commanders to evaluate space missions.

🚫 IMPOSSIBLE (0% CHANCE)
An event that CANNOT happen under the given conditions.
Example: Sound traveling through empty space vacuum without air molecules, or lighting a campfire without oxygen.
📉 UNLIKELY (LOW CHANCE)
An event with a small chance of happening (less than half).
Example: Picking the 1 damaged satellite channel out of 10 total channels (1 out of 10 = 10%).
⚖️ EQUALLY LIKELY (50/50 CHANCE)
Two or more outcomes that have the EXACT SAME chance of happening.
Example: Flipping a coin (Heads or Tails), or choosing between 2 docking ports where both are equally open.
📈 LIKELY (HIGH CHANCE)
An event with a high chance of happening (more than half).
Example: Selecting a clear navigation path when 9 out of 10 lanes are completely clear of asteroids (90%).
🎯 CERTAIN (100% CHANCE)
An event that is 100% guaranteed to happen under the given conditions.
Example: A spacecraft orbiting Earth experiencing Earth's gravitational pull, or solar panels producing zero power in a total solar eclipse.
